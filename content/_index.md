# Hi I'm Marek Novotný

I am an undergraduate social geography student at Charles University in Prague,
Czechia, where I focus on urban data science and spatial analysis.

I work at CZ.NIC z.s.p.o., the Czech domain registrar, where I am part of
the [Turris team](https://www.turris.com/en/). Here, I contribute to the
development of open-source routers and cybersecurity solutions.
I am part of **[Turris Sentinel](https://view.sentinel.turris.cz)**, an
internet security research project that monitors and analyzes attacks on
home networks.

In parallel, I’m part of the **[Urban Structure](https://uscuni.org/)**
research team at Charles University. Here, I help with managing two Debian
servers. My current project involves porting the [momepy](https://github.com/pysal/momepy)
Python library tools into QGIS GUI.

## Technical Toolkit

- **Spatial Analysis & GIS**: QGIS, PySAL, geopandas
- **Programming**: Python (spatial data science, Jupyter notebooks)
- **Databases**: PostgreSQL, Redis
- **Systems & DevOps**: Linux administration (Debian, Arch, OpenWrt), Docker
- **Data & Urbanism**: (Non)Spatial statistic, urban form, planning and development
