# Turris Sentinel - What has 2024 brought talk

My first public presentation at Installfest, Prague’s annual gathering for
GNU/Linux enthusiasts, was held on March 15, 2025, at the Czech Technical
University. Titled *Turris Sentinel – What has 2024 brought*, the talk
introduced the Turris Sentinel research project, an internet security
monitoring initiative from the Turris team that analyzes attack patterns on
home networks. I covered the project’s history, the anonymized data collected
from participating Turris router users, key findings and trends from 2024, the
geographical distribution of attacks, and the integration of IPv6 in December
2024, including a comparison of attacks over IPv4 and IPv6. Resources shared
included the [Turris Sentinel live security data visualization](https://view.sentinel.turris.cz)
and the [Turris Project open-source router platform](https://www.turris.com/en/).
